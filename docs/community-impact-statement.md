# Community Impact Statement – The Green Resourcerers LLC

## Mission

The Green Resourcerers LLC exists to reduce electronic waste in residential communities by removing obsolete satellite dishes and responsibly recycling the recovered materials, including precious metals.

## Environmental Impact

- Diverts satellite dish hardware from landfills
- Recovers copper, aluminum, and other metals for reuse
- Reduces visual blight in neighborhoods
- Promotes responsible e-waste practices

## Community Impact

- Provides free or low-cost removal for qualifying homeowners
- Creates local technician jobs
- Educates homeowners on recycling and environmental stewardship
- Partners with community organizations and local governments

## Measuring Impact

We track and report:
- Number of dishes removed
- Weight of materials recycled (lbs)
- Jobs created (full-time and part-time)
- Neighborhoods served
