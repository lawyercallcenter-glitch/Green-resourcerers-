# 18-Month Launch & Growth Timeline — The Green Resourcerers LLC

## Overview

| Phase | Months | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Phase 1: Foundation | 1–3 | Legal, tooling, hiring, pilot |
| Phase 2: Local Launch | 4–9 | Market entry, customer acquisition, 3–5 techs |
| Phase 3: Scale | 10–18 | Regional expansion, 10 techs, grant reporting |

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 1–3)

### Month 1
- [ ] File LLC articles of organization
- [ ] Open business banking account
- [ ] Secure general liability and workers' comp insurance
- [ ] Purchase first service vehicle (used cargo van)
- [ ] Source and purchase tool kits (safety harness, impact drivers, pipe cutters)
- [ ] Launch company website (React/Vite) with request form
- [ ] Deploy backend API (FastAPI) on cloud host

### Month 2
- [ ] Hire and onboard Technician #1 (OSHA 10 required)
- [ ] Establish relationship with certified recycling facility
- [ ] Complete 20-dish pilot in local neighborhood
- [ ] Gather testimonials and before/after photos
- [ ] Begin EPA e-waste recycler certification application

### Month 3
- [ ] Launch mobile app (Expo) for technician field updates
- [ ] Complete first full month of operations (40 dishes target)
- [ ] Submit recycler certification paperwork
- [ ] Refine scheduling and intake workflows
- [ ] Finalize grant application materials

## Phase 2 — Local Launch (Months 4–9)

### Month 4–5
- [ ] Hire Technicians #2 and #3
- [ ] Purchase second service vehicle
- [ ] Run digital ad campaign (Facebook / Nextdoor) targeting homeowners
- [ ] Partner with 2 HOAs for bulk removal agreements
- [ ] Target: 80 dishes/month

### Month 6
- [ ] Submit grant applications (EPA, USDA Rural Development, state environmental fund)
- [ ] Achieve 120 dishes/month milestone
- [ ] Issue first quarterly impact report (pounds recycled, CO₂ equivalent)
- [ ] Review and update SOPs

### Month 7–9
- [ ] Scale to 4–5 technicians
- [ ] Expand service area to 2nd metro area
- [ ] Negotiate volume pricing with recycling partner
- [ ] Target: 200 dishes/month
- [ ] Begin exploring B2B partnerships (property management firms, HOAs)
