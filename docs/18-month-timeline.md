## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–3)

**Objective:** Establish the legal, financial, and operational foundation for the business.

### Month 1 — Business Formation & Planning

- [ ] Register LLC with the state
- [ ] Obtain EIN from the IRS
- [ ] Open business bank account
- [ ] Finalize business plan and budget
- [ ] Begin insurance procurement (general liability, workers' comp, commercial auto)
- [ ] Research and identify required local/state permits and licenses
- [ ] Register on SAM.gov (for federal grant eligibility)

### Month 2 — Licensing, Equipment & Partnerships

- [ ] Obtain all required permits (recycling, waste transport, contractor license)
- [ ] Purchase or lease primary vehicle (truck or cargo van)
- [ ] Procure tools and safety equipment (ladders, harnesses, drills, PPE)
- [ ] Acquire material processing equipment (bins, shears, scales, testing kits)
- [ ] Identify and establish relationships with recycling facilities
- [ ] Identify and establish relationship with a precious metal refiner
- [ ] Set up accounting and CRM software
- [ ] Begin developing SOPs

### Month 3 — Branding, Training & Pre-Launch

- [ ] Finalize branding (logo, vehicle wrap, business cards, uniforms)
- [ ] Launch website and social media profiles
- [ ] Complete SOP documentation and safety training
- [ ] Conduct practice removals (personal property, volunteer sites)
- [ ] Establish material tracking and reporting systems
- [ ] Identify pilot area neighborhoods and begin outreach
- [ ] Apply for first round of grants (environmental, community development)

**Phase 1 Milestones:**
- ✅ LLC registered and insured
- ✅ All permits and licenses obtained
- ✅ Equipment procured and tested
- ✅ SOPs written and staff trained

---

## Phase 2: Pilot Operations (Months 4–6)

**Objective:** Launch operations in a defined pilot area, refine processes, and validate the business model.

### Month 4 — Soft Launch

- [ ] Begin accepting homeowner removal requests in pilot area
- [ ] Target: 10–20 removals in first month
- [ ] Process first batches of recovered materials
- [ ] Ship first batch to precious metal refiner
- [ ] Document all processes, times, costs, and yields
- [ ] Collect customer feedback

### Month 5 — Process Refinement

- [ ] Analyze pilot data: cost per removal, material yield, revenue per unit
- [ ] Refine SOPs based on field experience
- [ ] Adjust pricing if needed
- [ ] Begin outreach to HOAs, property managers, and municipal housing authorities
- [ ] Hire first additional technician (if volume supports it)
- [ ] Target: 20–30 removals

### Month 6 — Pilot Review & Planning

- [ ] Complete pilot area review and performance report
- [ ] Compile material recovery data and refine yield projections
- [ ] Update budget and financial projections with real data
- [ ] Identify expansion areas based on demand and density
- [ ] Submit grant progress reports (if applicable)
- [ ] Target: 25–40 removals

**Phase 2 Milestones:**
- ✅ 50–90 total removals completed
- ✅ Material recovery process validated
- ✅ Pricing model validated with real cost data
- ✅ First revenue from material sales received

## Phase 3: Expansion (Months 7–9)

**Objective:** Expand the service area, grow the team, and establish recurring revenue streams.

### Month 7 — Team Growth

- [ ] Hire 1–2 additional removal technicians
- [ ] Begin training new hires on SOPs and safety protocols
- [ ] Expand into second service area
- [ ] Establish relationships with additional recycling and refining partners
- [ ] Target: 40–60 removals

### Month 8 — Partnership Development

- [ ] Pursue contracts with HOAs, apartment complexes, and property management companies
- [ ] Approach municipal governments for bulk removal contracts
- [ ] Attend local business networking and environmental events
- [ ] Explore partnerships with roofing and home improvement companies for referrals
- [ ] Target: 50–70 removals

### Month 9 — Operational Scaling

- [ ] Evaluate need for second vehicle
- [ ] Optimize routing and scheduling for efficiency
- [ ] Implement inventory management for recovered materials
- [ ] Begin building a backlog of material for batch processing and sale
- [ ] Target: 60–80 removals

**Phase 3 Milestones:**
- ✅ Team expanded to 3–4 field staff
- ✅ Two or more active service areas
- ✅ At least one bulk or recurring contract signed
- ✅ 150–210 cumulative removals

## Phase 4: Full Operations (Months 10–12)

**Objective:** Reach full operational capacity in current service areas and launch a marketing push.

### Month 10 — Marketing Campaign

- [ ] Launch targeted digital marketing campaign (Google Ads, social media, Nextdoor)
- [ ] Distribute flyers and door hangers in target neighborhoods
- [ ] Issue press release about environmental impact to date
- [ ] Seek local media coverage (news segment, newspaper feature)
- [ ] Target: 70–100 removals

### Month 11 — Operational Maturity

- [ ] Streamline all processes for maximum efficiency
- [ ] Implement quality control audits on completed jobs
- [ ] Compile first annual environmental impact report
- [ ] Review and renegotiate vendor relationships (recyclers, refiners, disposal)
- [ ] Target: 80–110 removals

### Month 12 — Year-One Review

- [ ] Conduct comprehensive year-one financial review
- [ ] Analyze revenue vs. projections; identify gaps and opportunities
- [ ] Evaluate staffing levels and equipment needs for Year 2
- [ ] File annual grant reports
- [ ] Set goals and budget for Year 2
- [ ] Target: 80–110 removals

**Phase 4 Milestones:**
- ✅ Consistent monthly volume of 80+ removals
- ✅ Marketing generating inbound leads
- ✅ Year-one financial and impact reports completed
- ✅ 380–630 cumulative removals

## Phase 5: Scaling (Months 13–15)

**Objective:** Expand into additional service areas and diversify revenue streams.

### Month 13 — Geographic Expansion

- [ ] Identify and launch third and fourth service areas
- [ ] Hire additional technicians as needed
- [ ] Evaluate feasibility of a satellite storage/processing location
- [ ] Target: 100–130 removals

### Month 14 — Revenue Diversification

- [ ] Explore additional e-waste streams (old antennas, cable boxes, rooftop equipment)
- [ ] Investigate partnerships with telecom companies for decommissioning contracts
- [ ] Evaluate in-house precious metal processing vs. third-party refining cost/benefit
- [ ] Apply for additional grants based on demonstrated impact
- [ ] Target: 110–140 removals

### Month 15 — Systems & Infrastructure

- [ ] Invest in operations management software or upgrade existing systems
- [ ] Formalize employee handbook, benefits, and HR processes
- [ ] Conduct mid-year financial review and adjust projections
- [ ] Target: 110–140 removals

**Phase 5 Milestones:**
- ✅ Four or more active service areas
- ✅ Diversified revenue streams beyond core dish removal
- ✅ Scalable systems and processes in place
- ✅ 700–1,040 cumulative removals

## Phase 6: Optimization & Reporting (Months 16–18)

**Objective:** Optimize operations for profitability, complete grant reporting, and plan for long-term growth.

### Month 16 — Efficiency Optimization

- [ ] Analyze per-unit economics and identify cost reduction opportunities
- [ ] Optimize material recovery processes for higher yield
- [ ] Renegotiate vendor contracts based on volume
- [ ] Target: 120–150 removals

### Month 17 — Impact Reporting & Grant Closeout

- [ ] Compile comprehensive environmental impact report (total material diverted, metals recovered, landfill savings)
- [ ] Prepare and submit final grant reports with supporting documentation
- [ ] Document lessons learned and best practices
- [ ] Target: 120–150 removals

### Month 18 — Strategic Planning

- [ ] Conduct 18-month comprehensive business review
- [ ] Develop 3-year strategic growth plan
- [ ] Explore franchise or licensing model for other regions
- [ ] Evaluate acquisition of complementary businesses (e-waste recyclers, roofing companies)
- [ ] Prepare investor or lender package for growth capital (if needed)
- [ ] Target: 120–150 removals

**Phase 6 Milestones:**
- ✅ Optimized per-unit profitability
- ✅ All grant reports submitted and accepted
- ✅ 18-month impact report published
- ✅ 3-year strategic plan completed
- ✅ 940–1,490 cumulative removals
