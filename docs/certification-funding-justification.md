# Certification & Funding Justification – The Green Resourcerers LLC

## Purpose

This document supports grant applications and funding requests by outlining the certifications pursued, their relevance to operations, and the justification for associated costs.

## Certifications Sought

| Certification                        | Issuing Body              | Estimated Cost | Justification                                      |
|--------------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Small Business Certification (SBC)   | State / SBA               | $0–$200        | Qualifies for set-aside contracts and grants       |
| Minority/Women-Owned Business (MWBE) | State Agency              | $0–$500        | Access to targeted grant programs                  |
| EPA E-Waste Handler Registration     | EPA / State               | $0–$300        | Required for compliant e-waste transport/disposal  |
| OSHA 10-Hour Safety Certification    | OSHA-Authorized Trainer   | $150/technician| Demonstrates safety compliance; required for gov't contracts |

## Funding Sources Targeted

- EPA Environmental Justice Grants
- SBA Small Business Innovation Grants
- State-level recycling and environmental program grants
- Community Development Block Grants (CDBG)
- Local utility company sustainability programs

## ROI Justification

Each certification and grant application is projected to unlock $5,000–$50,000 in funding or contract eligibility, far exceeding the cost of acquisition.
