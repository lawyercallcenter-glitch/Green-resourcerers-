# Grant Application Materials — The Green Resourcerers LLC

## Narrative Section

### Organization Overview

The Green Resourcerers LLC is an environmental services company dedicated to the free
removal and responsible recycling of obsolete satellite dishes from residential properties
across the United States. Founded in 2025, our mission is to reduce electronic waste,
recover valuable materials, and create living-wage jobs in the green economy.

### Problem Statement

An estimated 35–40 million abandoned satellite dishes remain on American homes, the result
of cord-cutting and the shift to streaming services. These dishes represent:

- **A growing e-waste crisis** — aluminum, copper, and plastic-coated materials are
  landfilled when dishes are improperly disposed of
- **An economic barrier** — professional removal costs $75–$200, pricing out
  low-income homeowners
- **A community blight** — rusting dishes reduce property values and neighborhood
  aesthetics

No scalable, free residential removal service currently exists at the national level.

### Proposed Solution

The Green Resourcerers offers a **100% free removal service** for homeowners, funded
by the recovered scrap value of the removed materials. Our technology platform —
including a public-facing website, mobile technician app, and backend operations API —
enables us to track, schedule, and report every job with full accountability.

### Goals and Objectives

**Goal 1:** Divert electronic waste from landfills

- Objective 1.1: Remove and responsibly recycle 3,615 satellite dishes within 18 months
- Objective 1.2: Divert approximately 79,500 lbs (~39.75 tons) of material from landfills
- Objective 1.3: Achieve EPA-certified e-waste recycler status within 6 months

**Goal 2:** Create green economy employment

- Objective 2.1: Hire 10 full-time field technicians within 18 months
- Objective 2.2: Pay a minimum of $18/hour with benefits
- Objective 2.3: Prioritize veteran and underserved-community hiring

**Goal 3:** Serve homeowners equitably

- Objective 3.1: Provide service at no cost to 100% of residential customers
- Objective 3.2: Reach a minimum of 4 metropolitan service areas by Month 18
- Objective 3.3: Achieve a customer satisfaction score ≥ 4.5/5.0

### Evaluation Plan

The Company will measure impact using the following methods:

| Metric | Measurement Method | Frequency |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| Dishes removed | Job database (backend API) | Monthly |
| Material weight recycled | Technician field reports + recycling receipts | Per job |
| Jobs created | Payroll records | Monthly |
| Customer satisfaction | Post-service survey | Per job |
| CO₂ equivalent avoided | Material recovery calculations (EPA factors) | Quarterly |

### Sustainability

The Green Resourcerers is designed to be financially self-sustaining through material
recovery revenue. Grant funding will be used exclusively for startup costs (vehicle
purchase, equipment, certification, and initial staffing) that cannot yet be covered by
early-stage revenue. Once operational at scale (8+ technicians), the Company projects
positive monthly cash flow without further grant support.
