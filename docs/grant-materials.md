## 2. Project Narrative Template

### 2.1 Statement of Need

*[Customize the following to the specific community and grant program.]*

The [City/County/Region] faces a growing challenge of decommissioned satellite dish accumulation on residential properties. An estimated [number — research local data] homes in the service area have one or more abandoned dishes mounted on roofs, walls, or poles. These installations:

- Contribute to neighborhood blight, particularly in low-income communities where homeowners lack resources for removal
- Present structural and safety hazards as mounts corrode and dishes loosen over time
- Contain electronic components that will eventually enter the waste stream as e-waste
- Reduce property values and curb appeal, compounding economic challenges in already underserved neighborhoods

Current options for homeowners are limited and costly. General contractors and handymen typically charge $100–$300 for removal, with no material recovery or environmental benefit. Municipal waste services generally do not offer removal programs, and satellite TV providers have no obligation to retrieve decommissioned equipment.

There is a clear gap in the market for an affordable, environmentally responsible, and community-focused removal and recovery service.

### 2.2 Project Description

The Green Resourcerers LLC will implement a [number]-month program to remove decommissioned satellite dishes from homes in [target area], process recovered materials through responsible recycling channels, and create local employment opportunities.

**Program components:**

1. **Homeowner Outreach and Intake** — Targeted outreach through community organizations, neighborhood associations, housing authorities, and social services to identify and enroll homeowners. Priority enrollment for low-income residents, seniors, and residents of environmental justice communities.

2. **Professional Removal Services** — Trained, insured technicians remove dishes, mounts, and cabling following standardized safety protocols. All roof and wall penetrations are sealed and patched. Homeowners receive before/after documentation.

3. **Material Recovery and Recycling** — Removed components are sorted, processed, and directed to appropriate recycling and refining channels:
   - Metals (aluminum, steel, copper) → licensed scrap recyclers
   - Electronic components (circuit boards, LNBs) → certified precious metal refiners
   - Plastics → plastics recyclers
   - Non-recyclable residue → licensed disposal (minimized)

4. **Workforce Development** — Technicians and processing workers are recruited from the target community, trained in safety protocols and material handling, and provided with OSHA certifications. The program creates a pipeline for long-term employment as the business scales.

5. **Data Collection and Reporting** — All activities are tracked and documented to support impact measurement, grant reporting, and continuous improvement.

## 6. Sustainability Plan

### Financial Sustainability

The Green Resourcerers' business model is designed for long-term financial sustainability beyond the grant period through diversified revenue streams:

| Revenue Source | Description | Grant Period | Post-Grant |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|------------|
| Service fees | Residential and commercial removal fees | Partial (subsidized removals) | Primary revenue source |
| Material recovery | Sale of scrap metals and recyclable materials | Active | Active |
| Precious metal recovery | Revenue from refined precious metals | Active | Active; increasing with volume |
| Bulk contracts | HOA, municipal, and property management contracts | Developing | Growing revenue source |
| Additional grants | Environmental, workforce, community development | Applied/received | Continued pursuit |

**Path to self-sufficiency:**
- During the grant period, grant funds subsidize startup costs, equipment, and free-service programs
- Service fee revenue and material recovery revenue grow as operational volume increases
- By the end of the grant period, recurring revenue from services and materials is projected to cover [X%] of operating costs
- Post-grant, the business transitions to full self-sufficiency through a combination of service fees, material sales, and bulk contracts

### Operational Sustainability

- Standardized SOPs ensure consistent quality regardless of staff turnover
- Material tracking systems provide data-driven decision-making for route optimization and inventory management
- Ongoing workforce development creates a trained talent pipeline
- Equipment maintenance schedules and replacement planning ensure operational continuity

### Environmental Sustainability

- Continuous improvement in material recovery rates to maximize recycling and minimize landfill disposal
- Regular review of recycling and refining partners to ensure highest environmental standards
- Tracking and reporting of environmental metrics to maintain accountability
- Exploration of additional e-waste streams (antennas, cable boxes, rooftop equipment) to increase environmental impact per operational dollar

### Community Sustainability

- Established relationships with community organizations ensure continued outreach and access
- Free-service programs for vulnerable populations sustained through revenue from fee-paying customers and future grants
- Local hiring and advancement practices build long-term community investment in the business
- Published impact reports maintain transparency and community trust
