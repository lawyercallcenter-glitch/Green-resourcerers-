### Section 3: Assumption of Risk

The Owner acknowledges and understands that:

1. Satellite dish removal involves work at height, use of power tools, and manipulation of fasteners that may have been in place for many years.
2. Despite the exercise of reasonable care, there is an inherent risk of incidental cosmetic damage to roofing materials, siding, paint, or other exterior surfaces — particularly in areas where mounting hardware has penetrated the building envelope.
3. Pre-existing conditions such as aged roofing, deteriorated siding, corroded flashing, or prior improper installations may increase the risk of incidental damage during removal.
4. Weather conditions may require rescheduling for the safety of all parties.

The Owner acknowledges these risks and agrees that minor cosmetic damage incidental to the removal process (e.g., small paint chips, minor surface abrasions around former mounting points) does not constitute negligence on the part of the Company.

### Section 4: Release of Liability

In consideration of the services provided, the Owner, on behalf of themselves, their heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns, hereby **releases, waives, and discharges** the Company, its owners, officers, employees, agents, and subcontractors from any and all claims, demands, actions, or causes of action arising out of or related to the services performed under this agreement, **except** for claims arising from:

- Gross negligence or willful misconduct by the Company
- Damage to property unrelated to the agreed scope of work
- Violation of applicable laws or regulations by the Company

This release applies to, but is not limited to:

- Incidental cosmetic damage to roofing, siding, paint, or exterior surfaces at or near mounting points
- Pre-existing conditions discovered or revealed during the removal process
- Temporary disruption to satellite or cable television service

### Section 5: Indemnification

The Owner agrees to **indemnify, defend, and hold harmless** the Company, its owners, officers, employees, agents, and subcontractors from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, or expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising out of or related to:

1. The Owner's misrepresentation of property ownership or authorization
2. Undisclosed hazards or property conditions known to the Owner
3. The acts or omissions of the Owner, the Owner's family, tenants, guests, or agents during the performance of services
4. Third-party claims related to the Owner's property that are not caused by the Company's negligence
