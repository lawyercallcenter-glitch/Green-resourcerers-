# Homeowner Liability Waiver & Service Agreement
## The Green Resourcerers LLC

**PLEASE READ THIS AGREEMENT CAREFULLY BEFORE REQUESTING SERVICE.**

---

### Parties

This Liability Waiver and Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

- **Service Provider:** The Green Resourcerers LLC ("Company"), a limited liability company
- **Property Owner / Authorized Agent:** The individual completing the service request form ("Homeowner")

---

### 1. Description of Services

The Company agrees to provide the following services at no charge to the Homeowner:

- Professional removal of obsolete satellite dish(es) and associated mounting hardware
- Responsible disposal and/or recycling of removed materials
- Sealing of roof penetrations created by dish mounting hardware (where accessible)

The Company retains all removed materials. Homeowner relinquishes all ownership of
the satellite dish(es), mounting hardware, and associated cables at the time of removal.

---

### 2. Homeowner Representations and Warranties

By submitting a service request, the Homeowner represents and warrants that:

1. They are the owner of the property at the service address, or are an authorized
   representative of the property owner with authority to authorize this service.
2. They have the legal right to grant the Company access to the property and to
   authorize removal of the satellite dish(es).
3. The information provided in the service request is accurate and complete to the
   best of their knowledge.
4. They will provide reasonable access to the property on the scheduled service date.
5. The satellite dish(es) are not subject to any active lease, lien, or ownership
   dispute with a third party (e.g., a satellite TV provider).

---

### 3. Release and Waiver of Liability

**TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW**, the Homeowner hereby releases, waives,
and discharges the Company, its members, officers, employees, contractors, and agents
(collectively, "Released Parties") from any and all claims, demands, damages, costs,
expenses, and causes of action arising from or related to the performance of the
services, including but not limited to:

- Damage to roofing materials, gutters, siding, or landscaping incidental to removal
- Cosmetic changes to the property resulting from removal of dishes or hardware
- Cable disconnection or disruption of any existing service

This waiver does not apply to claims arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct
by the Company.

---

### 4. Property Damage Acknowledgment

The Homeowner acknowledges that:

- Removal of mounting hardware may leave visible holes, marks, or staining on roofing
  or siding materials
- The Company will make reasonable efforts to seal roof penetrations and minimize cosmetic
  damage, but cannot guarantee full restoration of surfaces
- Pre-existing damage or deterioration of roofing materials may be exposed during removal

---

### 5. Indemnification

The Homeowner agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any
third-party claims, including claims by satellite service providers or lienholders,
arising from the Homeowner's authorization of this service.

---

### 6. Authorization

By submitting the service request form (electronically or in paper form), the Homeowner:

- Acknowledges they have read and understand this Agreement
- Agrees to be bound by its terms
- Confirms they are 18 years of age or older
- Grants the Company permission to enter the property and perform the described services
