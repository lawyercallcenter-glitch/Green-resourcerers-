## 1. Job Intake & Scheduling

### 1.1 Homeowner Request Receipt
1. Request submitted via website form or mobile app.
2. Backend system creates record with status `pending`.
3. Admin reviews request within 1 business day.
4. Admin approves request and creates job; status changes to `approved` → `assigned`.
5. Technician receives job notification in mobile app.

### 1.2 Pre-Job Homeowner Communication
- Call or text homeowner 24 hours before scheduled visit to confirm appointment.
- Confirm number of dishes and access points.
- Remind homeowner to secure pets and provide gate/roof access if needed.
- If homeowner is unreachable after 2 attempts, flag job as `on_hold` and notify admin.

### 1.3 Scheduling Rules
- Schedule 4–6 jobs per technician per day (varies by dish count and access complexity).
- Cluster jobs geographically to minimize drive time.
- Allow 30-minute buffer between jobs.

## 2. Pre-Job Safety Checklist

Complete before arriving at every job site:

- [ ] Vehicle inspection (tires, lights, tools secured)
- [ ] Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) loaded:
  - Safety harness and lanyard (rooftop jobs)
  - Hard hat
  - Cut-resistant gloves
  - Safety glasses
  - High-visibility vest
- [ ] Ladder secured to vehicle
- [ ] First aid kit present and stocked
- [ ] Emergency contact list accessible
- [ ] Job details reviewed in mobile app (address, dish count, notes)

## 3. Dish Removal Procedure

### 3.1 On-Site Arrival
1. Park vehicle safely off the road; activate hazard lights if near traffic.
2. Greet homeowner and verify address and number of dishes.
3. Walk the property to identify all dish locations before starting.
4. Note any hazards: power lines, fragile roofing, unstable mounts.

### 3.2 Rooftop Removal
1. Inspect roof pitch and surface condition before ascending.
2. Attach safety harness to a secure anchor point — **never skip this step**.
3. Identify mount type: J-mount, tripod, chimney mount, or penetrating mount.
4. Remove mounting hardware, using penetrating oil for rusted bolts.
5. If lag bolts penetrate the roof deck, fill holes with exterior-grade sealant before leaving.
6. Lower dish and mounting hardware carefully — do **not** drop.

### 3.3 Ground / Pole-Mount Removal
1. Dig around pole base if necessary to expose anchor.
2. Cut or unscrew mounting pole at ground level.
3. Fill any holes with appropriate fill material (soil, concrete patch, as needed).

### 3.4 Cable Disconnection
1. Locate coax cable run from dish to home entry point.
2. Disconnect at dish end. Cap or seal entry point on home exterior.
3. Do **not** cut cables inside the home without homeowner permission.

## 4. Material Handling & Logging

### 4.1 On-Site Sorting
Separate materials into three categories:
- **Metal** (aluminum frame, steel mount hardware, copper coax center conductor)
- **Plastic / composite** (dish shell if fiberglass/ABS)
- **Electronics** (LNB head, any circuit boards)

### 4.2 Weight Logging
1. Weigh all materials using calibrated field scale before loading.
2. Record weights in the mobile app under Job → Material Details.
3. Take a photo of the scale reading for each material type.

### 4.3 Vehicle Loading
- Place heavy metal materials in the cargo area floor bin.
- Secure all loads — unsecured loads in transit are a safety and legal violation.

## 5. Job Completion & Reporting

1. Confirm with homeowner that site is clean and all holes/penetrations are sealed.
2. Obtain homeowner signature on the **Liability Waiver** (if not pre-signed).
3. Update job status to `completed` in the mobile app.
4. Enter final dish count and total material weight.
5. Add technician notes on any unusual conditions.
6. Return materials to recycling partner within 5 business days.
7. Obtain and upload recycling receipt to the job record.

## 6. Emergency Procedures

### Fall / Injury
1. Call 911 immediately.
2. Do not move an injured person unless in immediate danger.
3. Notify Operations Manager within 1 hour.
4. Preserve the scene for investigation.
5. Complete an incident report within 24 hours.

### Electrical Hazard
- If dish or cable appears to be live: **stop work immediately**, do not touch.
- Contact homeowner and advise them to call their utility company.
- Mark job as `on_hold` and notify admin.

### Vehicle Accident
1. Check for injuries; call 911 if needed.
2. Exchange insurance information.
3. Photograph the scene.
4. Notify Operations Manager immediately.
5. Do not admit fault at the scene.

## 7. Vehicle Maintenance

| Interval | Task |
|----------|------|
| Daily | Visual inspection (lights, tires, fluid levels) |
| Weekly | Interior clean-out; secure all tools |
| Monthly | Oil level, tire pressure, wiper blades |
| Every 5,000 miles | Oil change, tire rotation |
| Every 15,000 miles | Full service inspection |

All maintenance must be logged in the vehicle maintenance log (paper binder in each vehicle).
