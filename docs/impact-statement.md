# Impact Statement — The Green Resourcerers LLC

## Executive Summary

The Green Resourcerers LLC provides a free, professional satellite dish removal service
for homeowners while diverting significant quantities of electronic waste from landfills.
By recovering aluminum, copper, and other valuable materials, we create a self-sustaining
business model that benefits homeowners, communities, and the environment simultaneously.

---

## The Problem

### Electronic Waste Crisis
- The United States generates over **6.9 million tons** of e-waste annually (EPA, 2023)
- Satellite dishes contain aluminum frames, copper wiring, and plastic housings
- An estimated **35–40 million** obsolete satellite dishes remain installed on U.S. homes
  following the rise of streaming services
- Most are left in place because removal costs $75–$200 and no convenient free option exists

### Community & Aesthetic Impact
- Abandoned dishes lower property values and create neighborhood eyesores
- Improperly disposed dishes leach plastics and coated metals into soil and groundwater
- Low-income communities disproportionately bear the burden of e-waste accumulation

---

## Our Solution

The Green Resourcerers offers **free, certified removal** funded by material recovery revenue.

### How We Create Impact

| Action | Impact |
