# Environmental & Community Impact Statement

## The Green Resourcerers LLC

---

## Mission

The Green Resourcerers LLC exists to transform abandoned satellite infrastructure from an environmental liability into an economic and ecological asset. By removing decommissioned satellite dishes from homes and businesses and recovering the precious metals and recyclable materials within them, we reduce electronic waste, conserve natural resources, and create meaningful employment in the communities we serve.

---

## 1. Environmental Impact

### 1.1 Electronic Waste Reduction

Millions of satellite dishes have been abandoned on rooftops across the United States following the transition to streaming services and the discontinuation of legacy satellite TV providers. These dishes contain a mixture of metals, plastics, and electronic components that degrade over time, posing environmental risks.

**The Green Resourcerers directly addresses this problem by:**

- Removing decommissioned dishes before they deteriorate and shed materials into the environment
- Preventing microplastic and metal contamination from degrading dish housings and mounting hardware
- Diverting complete dish assemblies — including electronic components — from municipal waste streams

### 1.2 Precious Metal Recovery

Each satellite dish contains small but meaningful quantities of precious and base metals embedded in its electronic components (LNBs, circuit boards, connectors, and wiring):

| Material | Source Component | Environmental Benefit of Recovery |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Gold** | Circuit board traces, connector plating | Reduces demand for destructive gold mining operations |
| **Silver** | Solder, contact points | Conserves a finite resource used in solar panels and electronics |
| **Palladium** | Connector plating, multilayer ceramic capacitors | Reduces reliance on mining concentrated in ecologically sensitive regions |
| **Copper** | Wiring, coaxial cable, connectors | Offsets energy-intensive virgin copper smelting |
| **Aluminum** | Dish reflector body | Recycling aluminum uses 95% less energy than primary production |
| **Steel** | Mounting poles, brackets, hardware | Steel recycling reduces CO₂ emissions by ~58% vs. virgin production |

### 2.2 Property Improvement

Abandoned satellite dishes are a visual blight and a functional hazard. Their removal provides direct, tangible benefits to homeowners and communities:

- **Curb appeal:** Removing unsightly dish installations improves the exterior appearance of homes
- **Property value:** Clean rooflines and siding contribute to neighborhood property values
- **Roof integrity:** Removing old mounting hardware and sealing penetrations prevents water intrusion and rot
- **Safety:** Eliminates loose or corroded mounts that can fall during storms, posing injury risks
- **HOA compliance:** Helps homeowners meet homeowner association aesthetic standards

### 2.3 Hazard Removal

Aging satellite dish installations can present several hazards:

- **Structural risk:** Corroded mounts and brackets can loosen, causing the dish to detach during high winds
- **Electrical risk:** Improperly disconnected coaxial cables can carry residual charge or interfere with other systems
- **Pest harboring:** The sheltered space behind mounted dishes attracts nesting birds, insects, and rodents
- **Water damage:** Unsealed roof penetrations from mounting bolts lead to leaks and structural decay

Our removal process addresses all of these hazards and includes basic surface restoration.
