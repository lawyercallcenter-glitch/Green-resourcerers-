# Safety Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) – The Green Resourcerers LLC

## General Safety Requirements

- All technicians must wear appropriate PPE (gloves, safety glasses, hard hat) at all times on-site.
- No work to begin until a site safety assessment is complete.
- Technicians must never work alone on rooftop jobs.

## Satellite Dish Removal Procedure

1. **Site Assessment** – Confirm dish location, mounting type, and surrounding hazards (power lines, roof condition).
2. **PPE Check** – Verify all PPE is worn before ascending.
3. **Stabilization** – Secure ladder and establish roof anchor points where applicable.
4. **Disconnection** – Safely disconnect all cables before removal of hardware.
5. **Removal** – Unbolt and carefully lower the dish using appropriate rigging or team lift.
6. **Cleanup** – Remove all hardware, cable remnants, and debris from the site.
7. **Documentation** – Photograph before and after; log job completion in the app.

## Emergency Procedures

- In case of injury, call 911 immediately and notify the office.
- In case of electrical contact, do not touch the person; call 911.
- All incidents must be documented within 24 hours.

## Training Requirements

- All technicians complete safety orientation before first job.
- Annual refresher training required.
