# Capability Statement

## The Green Resourcerers LLC

---

### Company Overview

The Green Resourcerers LLC is a specialized environmental services company focused on the removal of decommissioned satellite dishes and the recovery of precious metals and recyclable materials from electronic waste. We bridge the gap between property remediation and responsible resource recovery, turning abandoned telecommunications equipment into economic and environmental value.

Our mission is to reduce electronic waste in landfills, recover valuable materials for reuse, and improve property aesthetics and safety — all while creating sustainable employment opportunities in underserved communities.

---

### Core Competencies

| Competency | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Satellite Dish Removal** | Safe, insured removal of residential and commercial satellite dishes, mounts, and associated cabling |
| **Precious Metal Recovery** | Extraction and refinement of gold, silver, palladium, and copper from circuit boards, LNBs, and connectors |
| **Environmental Recycling** | Responsible processing and recycling of plastics, aluminum, steel, and electronic components in compliance with EPA and state regulations |
| **Property Remediation** | Roof and siding repair patching after dish removal; restoration of mounting surfaces |
| **Community Outreach** | Free removal programs for low-income homeowners, seniors, and community housing organizations |

---

### Key Differentiators

- **End-to-End Service:** We handle the full lifecycle — from homeowner intake and scheduling through removal, material recovery, and environmentally compliant disposal.
- **Precious Metal Recovery Expertise:** Unlike general junk-removal services, we employ specialized processes to extract and refine precious metals, maximizing material value and minimizing waste.
- **Environmental Compliance:** All operations adhere to federal EPA guidelines, state environmental regulations, and local ordinances for e-waste handling and disposal.
- **Community-Centered Model:** We prioritize hiring from the communities we serve and offer reduced-cost or no-cost removal for qualifying residents.
- **Grant-Ready Operations:** Our documentation, tracking, and reporting systems are designed to meet the requirements of federal, state, and private grant programs focused on environmental remediation and community development.
- **Insurance & Licensing:** Fully licensed, bonded, and insured for residential and commercial property work.
